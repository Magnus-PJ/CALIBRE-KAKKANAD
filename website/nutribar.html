NutriBar

Food and drinks made on site, for people leaving a session or stopping in. The menu is not written, and the bar is not serving.

What the kitchen is meant to do

  • Protein food and healthy snacks or meals.
  • Shakes. Protein used in a shake is an ingredient, not a second retail sale.
  • Hydration and electrolyte drinks made here.
  • Broths and teas are candidates only. They are not on a menu.

A full hot kitchen is not assumed. The room needs washing, refrigeration, and a clear pickup. Packaged products stay on the retail shelf.
