RE-WYNDKOCHI

A calm place to recover, rebuild, and look after yourself. Physiotherapy, rehabilitation, recovery, doctor consultations, nutrition, a NutriBar, and a small retail shelf are planned together in Kakkanad, Kochi.

A plain note

Words such as physiotherapy, plunge, IV, and supplements on this site describe the plan. They are not a promise of treatment, a product recommendation, or an outcome. When the centre opens, services, clinicians, and a way to enquire will be published here.
