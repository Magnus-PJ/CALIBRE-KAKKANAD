How it should feel

Routine rooms stay simple and easy to clean. Feature finishes stay at reception and a few visible places. Cooling, light, power, water, and drainage are designed on their own. They are not solved by a decorative ceiling.

EMS has no permanent room until the system is chosen. A hyperbaric chamber, a second plunge, a pool, and premium body-composition equipment have no committed area.
