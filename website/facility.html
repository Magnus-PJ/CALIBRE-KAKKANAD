Coming soon to Kakkanad

The facility

A proposed second-floor centre of about 6,200 square feet in Kakkanad, Kochi. These rooms are a planning allocation. They are not a survey, and they are not a signed lease.

Rooms and what they are for

  • Reception and waiting, with a clear way in and the retail shelf nearby.
  • Six physiotherapy cubicles: M1, M2 and M3, and F1, F2 and F3.
  • An active rehabilitation gym for exercise, gait, and supervised progression.
  • A flexible movement studio. Pilates equipment is not selected.
  • Two doctor rooms, with space for basic assessment.
  • A nutrition consult room.
  • An IV lounge, nurse station, and secure storage. Not clearance to treat.
  • Plunge and sauna: one plunge and one sauna.
  • Red light and compression.
  • Changing, showers, lockers, and toilets.
  • NutriBar preparation and pickup.
  • Staff, linen, plant, storage, and circulation.

How it should feel

Routine rooms stay simple and easy to clean. Feature finishes stay at reception and a few visible places. Cooling, light, power, water, and drainage are designed on their own. They are not solved by a decorative ceiling.

EMS has no permanent room until the system is chosen. A hyperbaric chamber, a second plunge, a pool, and premium body-composition equipment have no committed area.
