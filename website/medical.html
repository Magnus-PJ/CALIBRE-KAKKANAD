Consultations

Set

Two private consultation rooms are in the plan, for assessment and review alongside physiotherapy and rehabilitation.

Not set

Clinicians are not appointed. A minor-procedure or ultrasound-guided option is unselected. Names of doctors will be published only after they are confirmed. No one can book a doctor here yet.

IV therapy

An infusion lounge, a nurse station, and secure storage are drawn into the plan. That space is not permission to start drips. Registration, staffing, emergency transfer, infection control, and consent have to come first.

People search for vitamin infusions, immunity drips, athletic recovery drips, NAD, and Myers cocktail infusions. Those named menus are a review list. They are not adopted prescriptions and they are not for sale.

Hyperbaric oxygen

A hyperbaric chamber is not part of the opening centre. There is no chamber, no price, and no session to book. It stays a later decision about fit and timing.
