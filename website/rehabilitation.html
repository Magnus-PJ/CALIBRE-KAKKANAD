Programmes in the plan

Set

The plan includes orthopaedic and post-operative programmes, sports injury rehabilitation, neurological and balance work, and hands-on recovery beside the gym.

Not set

Programmes are still being designed. Staffing is not confirmed, and nothing here can be booked. Return-to-sport testing is not selected.

After injury or surgery

Orthopaedic care, spine rehabilitation, joint replacement, and other post-operative programmes are intended. The gym needs open floor and clear space around equipment.

Sport and return to play

Sports injury rehabilitation covers knees, ankles, hamstring, Achilles, shoulder, and muscle strains from football, badminton, running, and the gym. Return-to-sport testing is a separate choice and is not selected.

Balance and neurological care

Stroke and neurological rehabilitation, gait, balance, and fall-prevention work are in scope. Women’s and postpartum programmes are included in the same planning group.

Hands-on recovery work

Sports massage, assisted stretching, and soft-tissue work are planned beside the gym. Myofascial release is an approach under that heading, not a booked treatment.

How a visit is meant to feel

Assessment first, then a programme you can follow, then a check on progress. Home physiotherapy, sports-club work, and memberships are later capacity questions. They are not offered today.
