Wellness

Longevity here means a programme you can stay with: a look at how you are, a plan, and a follow-up. It is not a product, a test kit, or a claim about biological age.

How a programme is meant to fit together

  • Basic assessment: body composition, posture, gait, grip, and function. Premium body-composition machines are deferred. VO2, RMR, force plates, and ECG are unselected.
  • A review of sleep, activity, stress, and daily habits, with medical and nutrition input when that care is actually staffed.
  • A package must not bill the same visit twice, once as a package and again as a separate component.
  • Workplace and executive programmes for Infopark are a later outreach idea. They are not a retainer you can buy.

Blood tests, if used, would be through an external laboratory. Genetic and epigenetic tests are not an opening offer.
