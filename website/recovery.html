Recovery

A quiet sequence after treatment or training: cold, heat, light, and compression. The rooms are planned. The equipment is not purchased, and sessions cannot be reserved.

Plunge and sauna

One cold plunge

The opening plan is one compact commercial plunge, also called an ice bath or cold-water immersion. A second plunge is not in the opening layout. Water temperature and brand are not set.

One sauna

One compact sauna is intended. The type, including whether it is infrared, is not chosen. It is not booked as a detox treatment.

Contrast recovery

Contrast means moving between the plunge and the sauna under a workflow still to be written. Changing rooms, showers, and lockers sit beside this area.

Dry recovery

  • Red light and near-infrared: one starter station. A premium full-body bed is not the opening specification.
  • Pneumatic compression for muscle recovery. The device and the number of bays are open. No brand is selected.
  • Sports massage, assisted stretching, and soft-tissue recovery. Capacity is not set.
  • Soreness after training, including delayed-onset muscle soreness, is a reason someone might use this area later. It is not a protocol on offer today.

Not part of the opening plan

Whole-body cryotherapy, a steam room, a hydrotherapy pool, and a second plunge appear in older notes. They are not opening purchases. If you were looking for those, this page is the honest place to land.
