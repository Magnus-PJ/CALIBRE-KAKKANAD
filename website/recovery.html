Plunge and sauna

Set

The opening plan is one compact plunge and one compact sauna, with contrast between them, one starter red-light station, and compression still to be specified.

Not set

The equipment is not purchased, and sessions cannot be reserved. Water temperature, sauna type, and device brands are not chosen.

One cold plunge

The opening plan is one compact commercial plunge, also called an ice bath or cold-water immersion. A second plunge is not in the opening layout. Water temperature and brand are not set.

One sauna

One compact sauna is intended. The type, including whether it is infrared, is not chosen. It is not booked as a detox treatment.

Contrast recovery

Contrast means moving between the plunge and the sauna under a workflow still to be written. Changing rooms, showers, and lockers sit beside this area.

Dry recovery
