Coming soon to Kakkanad

Physiotherapy

Hands-on care and guided exercise, in rooms that give you space. This is the clinical heart of RE-WYND KOCHI, planned for people in Kakkanad, Infopark, Ernakulam, and Kochi.

Six treatment rooms

The plan fixes six physiotherapy cubicles: three for men and three for women. Curtains can hide a bay from view. A confidential examination needs a closed room, and that requirement stays in the design.

Therapist numbers, device quantities, and the exact scope of practice are still to be set. The rooms are not open.

Care we intend to offer

Back, neck, and spine

Back pain, lower-back pain, neck pain, cervical spondylosis, sciatica, and slip-disc rehabilitation are planned physiotherapy themes. They are not a promise of a particular result.

Shoulder, knee, and wrist

Frozen shoulder, knee pain, and wrist problems such as carpal tunnel are part of the planned pain-care work.

Methods under review

Manual therapy, exercise therapy, taping, and electrotherapy such as IFT, TENS, ultrasound, and traction are options. Needling, shockwave, and laser are not selected.

Posture and desk strain

Posture review and care for people who sit long hours near Infopark are planned. Home visits and corporate ergonomics are later outreach, not a current service.

Where this leads

Exercise after the treatment room sits in rehabilitation. Recovery after effort sits in recovery. Search every theme on Find a service.
