# Development workstreams

Planning status: 7 October 2026.

These checklists expand the priority list in the README. They name the next decisions. They do not set prices, clinical protocols, or a launch date.

Complete the layout gates before locking equipment orders that depend on the shell. Complete the clinical gates before any public offer of medical or IV care.

## Layout and shell

- [ ] Measure usable area and confirm whether 6,200 sq ft is usable or chargeable.
- [ ] Record access, lift, entry, and egress on the actual floor.
- [ ] Test the [facility register](FACILITY-REGISTER.md) against that survey, including cubicle, gym, wet-area, and plant footprints.
- [ ] Confirm structural capacity for the selected plunge and sauna, not for a superseded two-pool suite.
- [ ] Confirm drainage, water, ventilation, waterproofing, power, and backup with an engineer. Figures copied from old blueprints are not approval.
- [ ] Confirm permitted use for a clinic of this type.
- [ ] Agree, in writing, what the building owner delivers as base building versus what the project must build.
- [ ] Keep circulation and service access free when rooms are fixed.

## Physiotherapy and rehabilitation

- [ ] Confirm assessments used at the first visit and at reassessment.
- [ ] Set therapist coverage for six cubicles, including separate male and female peaks.
- [ ] Finalize device quantities for cubicle care. Leave shockwave, laser, and needling unselected until chosen.
- [ ] Write programme outlines for orthopaedic, post-operative, sports, neurological, balance, and women's health pathways.
- [ ] Decide what share of therapist time can move to groups or home visits without breaking cubicle capacity.
- [ ] Provide a closed room for confidential examinations. Do not rely on curtains for that.

## Recovery

- [ ] Specify the single compact plunge and its services, filtration, and maintenance access.
- [ ] Select the sauna type and its ventilation and drainage needs.
- [ ] Specify the starter red-light station and the compression setup, including cleaning workflow.
- [ ] Size changing, showers, and lockers for the recovery sequence.
- [ ] Define supervision and what happens when equipment is down.
- [ ] Leave the second plunge, hydrotherapy pool, steam room, and whole-body cryotherapy off the opening layout.

## EMS

- [ ] Choose between local clinical EMS and a whole-body suit or training system, or record a decision to defer.
- [ ] Only after that choice, assign footprint, power, changing, storage, and supervision.
- [ ] Do not treat existing TENS or IFT equipment as the EMS decision.

## Medical and IV

- [ ] Define which medical services are actually in scope for opening.
- [ ] Name the practitioner model for two doctor rooms. Do not publish clinician names before they are confirmed.
- [ ] Complete establishment registration, staffing, emergency transfer, infection control, medicine control, biomedical waste, and consent requirements before IV.
- [ ] Treat any named infusion menu in old papers as a review list, not an adopted offer.
- [ ] Keep clinical and IV stock separate from retail stock.
- [ ] Leave minor-procedure and ultrasound-guided work unselected until explicitly chosen.

## Nutrition and wellness

- [ ] Specify dietitian consultations and the metabolic programme families.
- [ ] Design healthspan programmes so a package and its components are not billed twice for the same visit.
- [ ] Separate basic assessment from deferred premium body-composition equipment.
- [ ] Decide whether VO2, RMR, force-plate, jump-mat, or ECG options are in or out.
- [ ] Set follow-up and reassessment intervals before promoting a longevity programme.

## Food and retail

- [ ] Choose the NutriBar menu and a preparation model that does not assume a full hot kitchen.
- [ ] Classify every SKU once: packaged retail, prepared food, or clinical stock.
- [ ] Plan display, opening stock, replenishment, expiry, returns, storage, and POS.
- [ ] Separate food staffing from retail staffing where the work is different.
- [ ] Obtain the food and retail permissions that apply, and confirm tax treatment with a chartered accountant.

## Operations

- [ ] Unify reception, booking, and room readiness. The public site is not a booking system.
- [ ] Define clinical records. Keep patient data out of this repository.
- [ ] Set linen, cleaning, plant, and maintenance routines.
- [ ] Define home physiotherapy, corporate, and sports-club capacity only after in-centre hours are clear.
- [ ] Publish address, clinicians, services, and dates only after each is confirmed.
- [ ] Keep the public website and any internal planning tools separate.

## Before a public offer or opening

- [ ] Survey, structure, and building services accepted for the actual equipment.
- [ ] Written agreement on handover, deposit, fit-out period, and rent basis.
- [ ] Comparable quotations checked against one bill of quantities, including tax, freight, installation, and commissioning.
- [ ] Contingency and operating reserve left intact. Do not spend them to add deferred items.
- [ ] Clinical lead sign-off, commissioned equipment, permissions, and a cash runway review.
- [ ] No bookable calls to action on the public page until those gates are met.
