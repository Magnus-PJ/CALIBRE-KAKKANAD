## Rules

- Do not turn a deferred or review row into a room, a price, or a website offer by silence.
- Do not fund a deferred item by consuming contingency or operating reserve.
- Do not describe these rows as proven outcomes or as available services.
- When a row moves to `open` or is dropped, update this file, the scope index, and the README together.
