# Facility register

Planning status: 7 October 2026.

Services describe what a client may receive. This register describes the spaces those services need. **These areas are unmeasured planning allocations, not a survey, a signed lease, or a claim that the rooms fit.**

Source plans describe a proposed **6,200 sq ft second-floor shell in Kakkanad, Kochi**, adjacent to Suburban Club. That description is planning context. It is not evidence that the area is usable, that medical use is permitted, or that a lease is agreed. Confirm whether 6,200 sq ft is usable area or a super built-up / chargeable figure. If usable area is lower, revise this table before fixing rooms.

Older drawings that use a larger wet suite, two plunges, a full-body red-light pod, or a different cubicle count are superseded. Use this register with the [scope index](SCOPE-INDEX.md).

## Design rules that already apply

- Routine clinical and work areas use simple, cleanable, maintainable finishes.
- Feature finishes stay in reception and other selected visible areas. They do not spread across the whole floor.
- Curtains can give visual privacy in a treatment bay. They do not give acoustic privacy. Confidential examinations need a closed room.
- Cooling, lighting, power, wet-area services, drainage, and storage are design items of their own. Ceiling style does not settle them.
- Keep service access, circulation, and egress usable. Do not draw rooms across those routes.
- Select the EMS system before assigning it a permanent footprint. Until then, EMS has no separate row in this table.
- A hyperbaric chamber, a second plunge, a hydrotherapy pool, and premium body-composition equipment have **no committed area** in this schedule.
