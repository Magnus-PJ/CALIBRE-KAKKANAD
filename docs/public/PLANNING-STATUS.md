# Planning status

Date: 7 October 2026.

Calibre Kakkanad is a **proposed** integrated centre for physiotherapy, rehabilitation, recovery, wellness, longevity, medical consultation, IV therapy, nutrition, NutriBar food, and product retail. It is not open. Opening date, clinicians, bookable services, and contact details are not confirmed.

## What is fixed for planning

- One integrated centre and one client journey.
- Six physiotherapy cubicles: three male and three female.
- Two doctor rooms remain in the concept.
- Food preparation, packaged-product retail, and professional consultations stay separate operations.
- Simple finishes in routine areas; feature finishes only at reception and selected visible areas.
- Current recovery target: one compact plunge, one compact sauna, and one starter red-light station.
- A full hot kitchen is not assumed.
- The public website brand is RE-WYND KOCHI. Every page starts with “Coming soon to Kakkanad” and stays pre-opening until services and people are confirmed.

## What is not decided

- Measured shell, usable area, and landlord handover.
- Equipment brands, quantities, and quotations.
- Sauna type, EMS system, Pilates equipment, and advanced test devices.
- Clinical scope, permissions, and staffing for medical and IV services.
- Menu, retail range, stock, and sourcing.
- Memberships, outreach capacity, booking system, hosting, and launch date.
- Floor-plan image: expected and **not found**.
