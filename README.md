# CALIBRE KAKKANAD

## Integrated rehabilitation, recovery, wellness and longevity centre

A proposed integrated centre in Kakkanad, Kochi combining physiotherapy, rehabilitation, recovery, wellness, longevity, EMS, doctor consultations, IV drip therapy, nutrition, protein/healthy food, NutriBar, nutraceutical sales, supplement sales and packaged protein sales.

Planning status: 7 October 2026. Opening date, available services, practitioners and booking details are not yet confirmed. Equipment and programme choices remain under development.

## 1. Project principles

- One integrated centre and client journey.
- Six physiotherapy cubicles: three male and three female.
- Simple functional finishes, with selected feature treatment at reception and visible areas.
- Preserve the full intended service concept; emphasis is not a decision to remove or postpone a service.
- Treat food preparation, packaged-product retail and professional consultations as distinct operations.

## 2. Main focus and area-wise operating emphasis

The recommended emphasis is physiotherapy and rehabilitation as the clinical anchors, with recovery as a major complementary service. Medical, nutrition, wellness, food and retail functions complete the integrated concept. These emphasis levels describe attention and identity; they do **not** authorize removing or postponing modules.

| Area | Focus level | Main attention required | Measures to track when operating |
|---|---|---|---|
| Physiotherapy | Main clinical anchor | Six functional cubicles, therapist quality, assessment, privacy and continuity | Utilization, completion, reassessment and functional progress |
| Rehabilitation and performance | Main programme anchor | Active exercise, gait/balance, post-operative and sports rehabilitation | Attendance, progression and programme adherence |
| Recovery | Main complementary service | Integrated plunge/sauna, red-light, compression and recovery experience | Repeat use, utilization, downtime and running cost |
| Medical and IV | Clinical support and explicit service line | Two doctor rooms, clinical oversight and a properly specified IV operation | Appointments, staff coverage, service quality and actual costs |
| Wellness and longevity | Integrated programme identity | Assessment, medical review, nutrition, strength, recovery and follow-up | Retention, follow-up and repeat assessments |
| EMS | Explicit additional service | Define the system and role before purchase and space allocation | Supervised utilization, programme fit and total cost |
| Nutrition | Programme support and standalone consultations | Dietitian-led plans and ongoing follow-up | Consultations and programme adherence |
| NutriBar and protein food | Dedicated food operation | Menu, preparation, taste, consistency, storage and wastage | Sales, food/packaging costs, waste and repeat purchase |
| Nutraceutical, supplement and protein retail | Dedicated product-sales operation | Curated range, visibility, inventory, pricing and replenishment | Category sales, margin, stock turns and expiry |
| Reception and support | Operational foundation | Access, clear circulation, unified booking, maintainability and selected feature finishes | Wait times, room readiness and maintenance |

These are management measures, not validated demand or outcome forecasts.

Retail categories overlap: classify each SKU and count each transaction once. Protein used in a NutriBar shake is an ingredient cost, not another external retail sale. Plan display, opening stock, replenishment, expiry/returns, staffing, POS and storage. Clinical/IV inventory is distinct from customer retail stock.
