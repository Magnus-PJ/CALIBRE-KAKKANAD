# CALIBRE KAKKANAD

Proposed integrated rehabilitation and recovery clinic in Kakkanad, Kochi.

## Repository status
This repository was initialized on 7 October 2026. It is currently public. Confidential budgets, landlord negotiations and historical project plans are intentionally excluded.

## Project scope
One integrated clinic with six physiotherapy treatment cubicles: three male and three female. The intended service mix includes physiotherapy, rehabilitation, recovery, wellness, longevity, doctor-directed IV therapy, medical consultation, nutrition, protein and healthy food, NutriBar, and supplement/recovery retail. Source-listed equipment and additional options remain in the internal scope register with their original context; their inclusion is not a claim of launch readiness. Opening date and bookable services are not yet confirmed.
