# CALIBRE KAKKANAD

## Integrated rehabilitation, recovery, wellness and longevity centre

A proposed integrated centre on a second-floor shell in Kakkanad, Kochi. Source plans describe about 6,200 sq ft, with a catchment used only as planning context: Kakkanad, Infopark, SmartCity, and greater Ernakulam. That description is not a measured survey and not a signed lease.

The concept combines physiotherapy, rehabilitation, recovery, wellness, longevity, EMS, doctor consultations, IV drip therapy, nutrition, protein and healthy food, a NutriBar, and retail of nutraceuticals, supplements, and packaged protein.

Planning status: 7 October 2026. Opening date, available services, practitioners, and booking details are not confirmed. Equipment and programme choices remain under development.

## 1. Project principles

- One integrated centre and client journey.
- Six physiotherapy cubicles: three male and three female. Earlier drafts that use four cubicles, or ten bays, are superseded.
- Simple functional finishes, with selected feature treatment at reception and visible areas.
- Preserve the full intended service concept. Emphasis is not a decision to remove or postpone a service. Deferred items are named in the [advanced options](docs/public/ADVANCED-OPTIONS.md) list instead of being dropped in silence.
- Treat food preparation, packaged-product retail, and professional consultations as distinct operations.

## 2. Main focus and area-wise operating emphasis

The recommended emphasis is physiotherapy and rehabilitation as the clinical anchors, with recovery as a major complementary service. Medical, nutrition, wellness, food, and retail functions complete the integrated concept. These emphasis levels describe attention and identity. They do **not** authorize removing or postponing modules.

| Area | Focus level | Main attention required | Measures to track when operating |
|---|---|---|---|
| Physiotherapy | Main clinical anchor | Six functional cubicles, therapist quality, assessment, privacy, and continuity | Utilization, completion, reassessment, and functional progress |
| Rehabilitation and performance | Main programme anchor | Active exercise, gait and balance, post-operative and sports rehabilitation | Attendance, progression, and programme adherence |
| Recovery | Main complementary service | One compact plunge, one compact sauna, starter red-light, compression, and the recovery experience | Repeat use, utilization, downtime, and running cost |
| Medical and IV | Clinical support and explicit service line | Two doctor rooms, clinical oversight, and a properly specified IV operation | Appointments, staff coverage, service quality, and actual costs |
| Wellness and longevity | Integrated programme identity | Assessment, medical review, nutrition, strength, recovery, and follow-up | Retention, follow-up, and repeat assessments |
| EMS | Explicit additional service | Define the system and role before purchase and space allocation | Supervised utilization, programme fit, and total cost |
| Nutrition | Programme support and standalone consultations | Dietitian-led plans and ongoing follow-up | Consultations and programme adherence |
| NutriBar and protein food | Dedicated food operation | Menu, preparation, taste, consistency, storage, and wastage | Sales, food and packaging costs, waste, and repeat purchase |
| Nutraceutical, supplement, and protein retail | Dedicated product-sales operation | Curated range, visibility, inventory, pricing, and replenishment | Category sales, margin, stock turns, and expiry |
| Reception and support | Operational foundation | Access, clear circulation, unified booking, maintainability, and selected feature finishes | Wait times, room readiness, and maintenance |

These are management measures, not validated demand or outcome forecasts. Running cost and margin are tracking ideas for later operations. This README does not publish budgets.

## 4. Facilities and area-wise design focus

The [facility register](docs/public/FACILITY-REGISTER.md) lists planning areas that add up to 6,200 sq ft, including circulation. Those figures are unmeasured allocations. They are not a claim that the rooms fit the real shell.

| Facility / area | Main purpose | Design and operating focus |

| Male physiotherapy cubicles | Three treatment spaces (M1–M3) | Privacy, therapist access, and a practical treatment workflow |
| Female physiotherapy cubicles | Three treatment spaces (F1–F3) | Privacy and a closed room where an examination must stay confidential |

| Thermal recovery | One plunge, one sauna, and contrast recovery | Wet and dry movement, services, maintenance, and changing access |
| Red-light and compression area | Dry recovery | One starter station, equipment access, and cleaning |
| Changing, showers, and lockers | Recovery support | Privacy, practical movement, and storage |

Use simple, maintainable finishes in routine clinical and work areas. Concentrate decorative features in selected visible areas. Functional cooling, lighting, power, wet-area services, and storage need design attention independently of ceiling style.

## 5. Sales categories and accounting separation

| Activity | Examples retained in scope | Revenue and cost treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Packaged protein sales | Whey, plant protein, and selected protein products | Retail sale and purchase cost |
| Nutraceutical sales | Selected range. Source examples include vitamins, omega-3, collagen, and joint-support products | Retail sale and purchase cost |
| Supplement sales | Creatine, electrolytes, magnesium, and selected sports or health supplements | Retail sale and purchase cost |
| Recovery accessories | Bands, rollers, braces, insoles, and compression garments | Retail sale and purchase cost |
| NutriBar / prepared food | Prepared shakes, drinks, snacks, and healthy or protein food | Food sales. Ingredients, packaging, preparation, and waste |
| Dietitian consultations | Counselling and nutrition programmes | Professional or programme income |

Retail categories overlap. Classify each SKU and count each transaction once. Protein used in a NutriBar shake is an ingredient cost, not another retail sale. Plan display, opening stock, replenishment, expiry and returns, staffing, POS, and storage. Clinical and IV inventory is distinct from customer retail stock.

Example product names above are planning categories, not brand endorsements.
